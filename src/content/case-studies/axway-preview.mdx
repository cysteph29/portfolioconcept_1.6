<Section id="problem" sections={frontmatter.sections}>

<h2 className={openingStyles.statement}>Finding why an invoice failed took eight steps.</h2>

<p className={openingStyles.support}>Operations handled roughly 150 failed invoices a day, with a 24-hour resolution window. To diagnose a failure, users matched an email notification’s timestamp to a task run, then navigated through subtasks and invoices to reach the error details.</p>

<ImagePlaceholder label="Original eight-step diagnosis flow" />

</Section>

<Section id="invoice-list" divided sections={frontmatter.sections}>

<h2 className={openingStyles.statement}>Failed invoices became visible directly in the list.</h2>

<p className={openingStyles.support}>The original invoice list showed neither status nor timestamps. I surfaced both in the redesigned list so users could identify failed invoices without tracing the underlying task runs.</p>

<ImagePlaceholder label="Invoice list — status and timestamps" />

</Section>

<Section id="search" divided sections={frontmatter.sections}>

<h2 className={openingStyles.statement}>The invoice list could be searched by the notification’s timestamp.</h2>

<p className={openingStyles.support}>Instead of remembering an email’s time and searching through task runs, users could search invoices directly by timestamp. Reference-number search supported customer enquiries, while clearer filters helped narrow the results.</p>

<ImagePlaceholder label="Search and filters" />

</Section>

<Section id="details" divided sections={frontmatter.sections}>

<h2 className={openingStyles.statement}>Error details were one click away.</h2>

<p className={openingStyles.support}>A Details action opened the invoice’s status and error information in a drawer directly from the list, bypassing the intermediate technical screens.</p>

<ImagePlaceholder label="Invoice details drawer" />

</Section>

<Section id="outcome" divided sections={frontmatter.sections}>

<h2 className={openingStyles.statement}>Invoice diagnosis took 67% less time in testing.</h2>

<p className={openingStyles.support}>We compared task completion times for the original and redesigned workflows with Operations team members who had participated in earlier research. The full solution—including the redesigned list, details drawer, search, and filters—reached implementation.</p>

<ImagePlaceholder label="Outcome visual — 67% reduction in diagnosis time" />

</Section>
