## Users: bank and credit-union buyers, weighing control before capability.

The reader I designed for is a bank or credit-union buyer deciding whether to let agents move real money. I audited the site as that buyer would, and wrote down every place it asked for trust before it had earned any.

I did not test with real bank buyers. The audit stands in for that, and it is the main limit of this study.

- **Bank and credit-union buyers:** Weigh control, proof and risk before capability.
- **Their customers and members:** Already ask AI about money, inside or outside the bank.

3. Approval required

### Audit: what I found and what I did

1. **Before:** One friendly voice for everything. **Redesign:** Two voices: a serif for the signed statement, a mono for the system record.
2. **Before:** Decorative mountain art on the first screen. **Redesign:** The control process is the hero image: request, policy check, approval, audit log.
3. **Before:** Rules, audit trail and kill switch in the last third. **Redesign:** Moved up to the fourth section, on their own teal stage.
4. **Before:** Announcement bar, pop-up and cookie banner before the headline. **Redesign:** Nothing between the visitor and the headline; the pop-up is dropped.
5. **Before:** A segment choice instead of an action. **Redesign:** One primary action, “Meet your agent”, in the header and the hero.
6. **Before:** Pop-up clips the email field on phones. **Redesign:** One column on phones, form fields at least 44px tall, no overlays.

### Palette

- `#F3F3F0` — **Paper** — Page
- `#171717` — **Ink** — Text
- `#0E3D42` — **Teal** — Trust
- `#2F8A8C` — **Mid teal** — Focus
- `#D4B3CA` — **Mauve** — Warmth
- `#F4FF45` — **Signal yellow** — Signal only

Yellow is a bar, a digit or a strip. Never a fill.

### Typography

- **Display · Newsreader** — `'Newsreader', 'Times New Roman', serif` — 46px — “We deploy AI agents that handle money.”
- **Body · Geist** — `'Geist', sans-serif` — 17px — “Agents that carry out real banking transactions for your customers, under policies, approvals and audit trails you control.”
- **Record · Geist Mono** — `'Geist Mono', monospace` — 12px — “PM-2026-001 // VERSION 1.0 · REQUESTED → CHECKED → APPROVED → LOGGED”
