Selecting Details in an invoice row opens a drawer from the right. Operations can inspect the error information while keeping the Documents list in view.

<div className="case-study-pair">

## Users relied on an email timestamp and Ctrl+F in the task screen.

Documents had no search bar. Operations took the timestamp from a failure-notification email and used the browser’s Find function in the task screen to locate matching information.

<AxwayFindWorkaround />

## A new search bar let them use that timestamp directly in Documents.

I introduced search in Documents that supports timestamps and invoice references, bringing that investigation into the invoice list.

<ImagePlaceholder label="After — use the notification timestamp in the new Documents search bar" ratio="16/9" />

</div>
